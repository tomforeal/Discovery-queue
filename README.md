# Discovery-queue
The Discovery Queue is a local-first media discovery and backlog app for movies, TV, books, comics, games, and more. It remembers what you’ve saved, finished, skipped, or declined so recommendations don’t keep repeating themselves.

The app combines your personal Library with smart backlog picks, genuinely new discoveries, Universal Search, Collections, imports, detailed Media Hubs, and The Librarian — an in-app curator who reviews recommendations and has his own mysterious Archive lore.

Discovery Queue also supports separate family profiles, shared Family Night profiles, a Family Shelf, Shared Family Queue, private profile history, and household-aware recommendations without merging everyone’s individual tastes.

Your media history stays stored locally and remains the source of truth. AI is optional and replaceable. Users who want AI-powered discovery can connect their own OpenRouter API key and use the free-model router.

**The database remembers you. The AI is replaceable.**
