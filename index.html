👨‍👩‍👧 Family Accounts

Each profile has its own Library, history, imports, recommendations and Librarian memory. Special profiles like Family Night can read selected signals from chosen family members while keeping their own history.

Local-first accounts
Profiles and PINs live on this device. A PIN keeps casual family switching private, and profiles can hide detailed history/reactions from household views. Browser storage is still not a secure encrypted vault. Your free-AI key, quota guard and cooldown are device/household-wide; personal media data stays profile-specific. Cloud accounts can later use the same profile IDs without changing your Library schema.

How AI behaves

Discovery Queue uses its local discovery reservoir and successful-response cache before making a new AI request. Temporary failures retry with backoff. Rate limits trigger a shared cooldown. Daily-quota errors stop network retries until reset. Local Library/history features keep working.

🎚 Discovery Chaos

Only affects generic recommendations. Your explicit request always wins.

📚 The Librarian

The Librarian is the archive's recurring reviewer. His media opinions come from AI, but his personal lore does not. His hidden backstory is canonical local data, so changing AI models cannot rewrite his history.

Every so often he lets something slip. The app remembers those remarks permanently. He also keeps restrained, action-based notes about your archive habits and can occasionally append a post-completion footnote to an old review.

🧩 Characters + Lore

This controls media/franchise lore in future deep-context passes. It does not expose The Librarian's hidden canon.

⚙️ Efficient Core

Local reservoir → local cache → shared cooldown → free AI refill. A successful refill can save extra discoveries for future zero-call sessions. Media Hub deep context is delayed briefly and cached after success.
